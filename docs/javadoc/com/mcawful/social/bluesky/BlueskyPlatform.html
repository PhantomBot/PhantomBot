Class BlueskyPlatform

java.lang.Object
com.mcawful.social.bluesky.BlueskyPlatform
All Implemented Interfaces:
SocialPlatform
public final class BlueskyPlatform extends Object implements SocialPlatform
Bluesky, through the AT Protocol

Polls:

  • mentions: mention, reply and quote notifications
  • reposts: repost notifications, which also feed repost rewards
  • posts: the linked account's own posts, without replies, reposts and the posts the bot made
Notifications are never marked as read, so they stay unread in the Bluesky app.
